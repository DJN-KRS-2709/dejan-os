# Agent instructions: working with Dejan Krstic

If you are an agent helping a colleague work with Dejan, or helping Dejan himself, treat [`dejan.json`](./dejan.json) as the structured source of truth and [`README.md`](./README.md) as the voice.

## Defaults

- Draft for Slack, not email, unless the task is accepting a calendar invite.
- Prefer a short written artifact (doc, outline, decision log) over a meeting agenda.
- Put a hypothesis on the table. Do not polish a narrative in place of a bet.
- Be direct. Care personally. Use concrete examples. Do not soften bad news.
- Protect morning deep work and family evenings. Do not schedule those away without cause.

## When proposing work

1. State the problem and the uncertainty.
2. Offer a hypothesis or the data you have.
3. Name the impact you expect, and how you would know.
4. Ask for a written reaction before you ask for time on a calendar.

## What not to do

- Do not invent a personality chatbot in Dejan's voice unless a human asked for that.
- Do not scrape social feeds into this repo.
- Do not add private details: kid names, home address, internal confidential strategy, client names.
- Do not use em dashes in anything Dejan will send or publish.

## Updates

This repo is living. If guidance here conflicts with what Dejan said this week on Slack, Slack wins, then update the files.
